From September 2004 to August 2007, I taught computer science at Moreau Catholic High School. My courses included AP Computer Science, Intro to Computer Science, and Computer Graphics.

Teaching gave me an early appreciation for making technical ideas accessible—an approach I’ve carried into developer education, partner integrations, and engineering leadership.
