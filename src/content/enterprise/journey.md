## Why Enterprise AI?

The AI space is moving fast. Tools that felt experimental six months ago are now production-ready, and the gap between "can this work?" and "will someone pay for this?" is narrowing every day. This section is where I document my honest journey trying to figure that out.

## Starting Point

I'm approaching this from an engineering background — I understand the technology. What I'm working to understand is the business side: identifying real problems worth solving, finding the right customers, and building something sustainable.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Strategy** — How I'm thinking about positioning and market fit
- **Market Research** — Who's buying AI solutions and what they actually need
- **Products** — The things I'm building and testing
- **Lessons Learned** — What's working, what isn't, and what I'd do differently

## The Honest Version

This is not a success story — not yet. It's a real-time log of experiments, pivots, and hard-won lessons. If you're on a similar path, I hope something here saves you time.
