## The Landscape

AI adoption in the enterprise is uneven. Large tech-forward companies are already deep in it. The long tail — small businesses, regional firms, traditional industries — is still figuring it out. That's the gap.

## What Buyers Actually Care About

Based on conversations and research, the top concerns from decision-makers considering AI tools are:

1. **Data privacy** — Will my data be used to train models? Who can see it?
2. **Reliability** — What happens when it's wrong? How often is it wrong?
3. **Integration** — Does it work with what I already have?
4. **ROI clarity** — Can you show me a number?

These are solvable problems, but they require a different kind of pitch than "look how smart this model is."

## Industries with the Most Traction

| Industry | Use Case | Willingness to Pay |
|----------|----------|-------------------|
| Legal | Document review, contract analysis | High |
| Real estate | Lead qualification, listing descriptions | Medium |
| Healthcare admin | Prior auth, billing codes | High |
| E-commerce | Product descriptions, support tickets | Medium |
| Financial services | Report summarization, compliance checks | High |

I'm currently exploring two verticals: **legal tech** (specifically small-firm document workflows) and **real estate** (AI-assisted CRM and lead follow-up). Early conversations have been promising in both.
