## Philosophy

Every product I build starts as a question: "Would someone pay for this tomorrow?" If the answer is maybe, I build the simplest possible version and find out. No six-month builds before talking to customers.

## Active Experiments

### AI Document Assistant
A lightweight tool for small law firms to ask questions about uploaded contracts and legal documents using RAG. Currently in early access with two firms.

**Status:** Active — collecting feedback  
**Tech:** Astro frontend, Python/FastAPI backend, pgvector, Claude API  
**Hypothesis:** Small firms can't afford enterprise legal AI but need the capability

### Lead Follow-Up Automator
An AI-powered follow-up sequence builder for real estate agents. Connects to CRM, drafts personalized outreach based on lead history and property interest.

**Status:** Prototype — needs more validation  
**Tech:** Node.js, OpenAI API, HubSpot integration  
**Hypothesis:** Agents lose deals because follow-up is inconsistent — AI makes it effortless

## Graveyard

Things I built and stopped:

- **Generic chatbot builder** — Too crowded, no clear differentiator
- **AI meeting summarizer** — Solved a real problem but the market is saturated

## Lessons

The best ideas come from customer conversations, not brainstorming sessions. Every experiment I've shelved was something I thought was clever without enough validation.
