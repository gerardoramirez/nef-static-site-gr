NASA’s [Adopt-a-Pixel](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/roman-space-telescope/adopt-a-pixel/) program for the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope is a small, public way to take part in the first images from a telescope built to map dark energy, exoplanets, and infrared sky.

The certificate names me as steward of pixel **00107572**. The accompanying note is the part that stuck:

> Though small, this pixel carries its share of a much larger picture — a fragment of light captured by NASA and now entrusted to a caring steward. Wherever it sits in the frame, it helps complete the view.

You can adopt your own pixel on NASA’s program page.
