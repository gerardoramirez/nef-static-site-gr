Some of the most interesting work begins when nobody quite knows what the answer is.

Throughout my career, I’ve worked on problems where requirements were incomplete, systems were complicated, teams had different perspectives, and the path forward had to be discovered.

This talk explores how to move from ambiguity to clarity by connecting customer needs, product thinking, architecture, and engineering.

It’s about learning to find the real problem before racing toward the solution.
