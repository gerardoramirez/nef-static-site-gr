What if we started with people instead of features?

As AI makes it dramatically easier to build software, our ability to create is beginning to outpace our ability to understand what should be created.

Human Truth Driven Design explores a different starting point.

Instead of moving directly from problem → feature, we begin by understanding the human truth underneath the problem and allow that truth to guide product principles, experiences, interactions, interfaces, and ultimately technology.

This talk explores how teams can use human truths as a foundation for making better product decisions.
