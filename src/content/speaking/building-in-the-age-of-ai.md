Building software is becoming easier. Building something meaningful isn’t.

AI is changing who can build software, how quickly ideas can become products, and what we expect from engineers and product teams.

But faster development doesn’t automatically produce better products.

I explore what happens when the cost of building approaches zero, why judgment and product thinking become increasingly valuable, and why understanding the people we build for may become one of the most important skills in technology.
