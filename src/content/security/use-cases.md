## Enterprise Applications

- **API Security:** Authenticate machine-to-machine communications between microservices
- **Zero Trust Architecture:** Verify every connection regardless of network location
- **Service Mesh:** Secure service-to-service communication in Kubernetes (e.g., Istio, Linkerd)
- **VPN Access:** Authenticate remote users and devices

## IoT and Device Management

- **Device Authentication:** Verify IoT devices connecting to cloud services
- **Manufacturing:** Embed certificates during device production for supply chain security
- **Over-the-Air Updates:** Ensure only authorized devices receive firmware updates

## Financial Services and Compliance

- **Banking APIs:** Secure payment processing and account access
- **Regulatory Compliance:** Meet requirements for PCI DSS, SOC 2, and other standards
- **Trading Systems:** Authenticate high-value transactions

## Government and Healthcare

- **HIPAA Compliance:** Protect patient health information
- **Government Systems:** Secure classified and sensitive government communications
- **Electronic Identity:** Digital IDs for citizens and government employees
