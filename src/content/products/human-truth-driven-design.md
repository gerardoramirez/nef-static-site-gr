Human Truth Driven Design is a framework for making people the starting point of product creation.

As AI makes it easier to build software, the ability to create can outpace our understanding of what should be created. This framework begins with the human truth underneath a problem, then uses that truth to guide product principles, experiences, interactions, interfaces, and technology.

The goal is to help teams make better product decisions by understanding people before deciding what to build.
