## Overview

Equipo Azteca is a futbol blog dedicated to the Mexican national team. It serves as a personal playground for experimenting with agentic blogging—where AI-assisted workflows power content creation, curation, and publishing around the beautiful game.

## Focus

- Coverage of El Tri: matches, players, and tournaments
- Agentic content workflows and automation
- A passion project born from a love of futbol

## Technology

Agentic blogging pipeline with AI-assisted content generation and publishing.
